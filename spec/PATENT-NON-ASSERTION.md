# PRML — Patent Non-Assertion Grant (restated)

> **Status: draft, unsigned.** This restated grant takes effect on signature; the date will be recorded here. Until then the covenant of 8 May 2026 and the patent licence in the Community Specification License 1.0 (see LICENSE.md, Section 2) apply.

**PRML — Pre-Registered ML Manifest Specification · Falsify OÜ · September 2026**

This grant accompanies the PRML specification (working draft v0.1, published at spec.falsify.dev/v0.1 under the Community Specification License 1.0, with its reference implementations under the MIT License) and any subsequent version of the same specification published by Falsify OÜ or by any organisation to which stewardship of the specification is transferred.

## Relationship to earlier covenants

The authors of the specification issued a patent non-assertion covenant of the same scope on 8 May 2026 under the name of the specification's original publisher. That covenant remains in force for its own scope. This document restates the grant in the name of Falsify OÜ and of the author personally, so that the covenant does not depend on the continued existence or legal succession of any single company. It does not narrow any earlier grant.

## Grant

Falsify OÜ (registry code 17574308, Tallinn, Estonia) and Cüneyt Öztürk, in his personal capacity as author of the PRML specification (together, "the Grantors"), hereby grant a worldwide, royalty-free, irrevocable, non-sublicensable, non-exclusive licence to all implementers and users of the PRML specification under any patent rights, present or future, that either Grantor holds or comes to hold over: (a) the manifest schema, (b) the canonical byte serialisation algorithm, (c) the registry anchor protocol, including sealed commitments and reveal, or (d) any combination thereof, solely to the extent required to implement, distribute, or use any version of the PRML specification published under an open licence.

The Grantors reserve no patent rights against any conforming implementation of PRML.

## Scope

The grant applies to all conforming implementations of PRML v0.1 and to any future version of the specification that maintains hash-equivalence for v0.1-shaped manifests (v0.2, whose request for comments closed on 22 May 2026, satisfies this property by design; the v0.3 backlog is bound by the same condition).

The grant covers the four published reference implementations (Python, JavaScript, Go, Rust, all under the MIT License) and any third-party implementation of the specification, regardless of programming language or distribution model. Use cases include, without limitation, academic research, commercial products, regulatory compliance tooling, audit and assurance practices, insurers, testing and certification bodies, and standards-body reference implementations.

## Transfer of stewardship

If stewardship of the specification is transferred to a foundation, consortium or standards body, this grant continues to bind the Grantors with respect to the transferred specification and all versions derived from it. The Grantors will cooperate in restating the grant in whatever form the receiving organisation requires.

## Termination

This grant terminates automatically with respect to any party that initiates patent litigation alleging that any conforming implementation of PRML infringes a patent held by that party. Termination is per-grantee; the grant remains in effect for all other parties.

## No warranty

This grant does not constitute a warranty regarding the validity, existence or scope of any patent. It is a unilateral covenant by the Grantors not to assert patents against conforming implementations. No patent applications covering the subject matter have been filed by either Grantor as of the date below.
